FIVE-LESSON PATHWAY

Confidence, built step by step.

1. Meet the assistant

Understand conversational AI and why important facts still need verification.

2. Prompting Without Fear

Use Goal + Context + Constraints + Output to make requests clearer.

3. Conversation Is the Superpower

Improve answers through follow-up questions instead of chasing a perfect prompt.

4. Voice & Accessibility

Use voice or dictation when available while protecting private information.

5. Put AI to Work

Complete a useful real-world task, verify important information and review the result.
