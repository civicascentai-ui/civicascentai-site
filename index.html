Our mission

CivicAscent AI exists to make artificial intelligence simple, practical, and free to learn for our community.

That means three things. Simple: no jargon, and no technical talk. If a neighbor cannot follow it, we rewrite it. Practical: every lesson is about something you can actually use, such as writing a letter, planning a budget, helping with homework, or running a small business. Free to learn: our community classes always cost nothing. Nobody pays to learn.

Who we serve

We teach everyday people who want to use AI in daily life and do not know where to start. That includes neighbors, veterans, older adults, students, workers, families, small business owners, and low-income people and communities. You do not need a technical background to begin.

Some pages on this website are also available in Spanish. There is a page for veterans and military families, and you can register interest in a free workshop. Community classes are launching December 2026.
