Our mission

CivicAscent AI exists to make artificial intelligence simple, practical, and free to learn for our community.

That means three things. Simple: no jargon, and no technical talk. If a neighbor cannot follow it, we rewrite it. Practical: every lesson is about something you can actually use, such as writing a letter, planning a budget, helping with homework, or running a small business. Free to learn: our community classes always cost nothing. Nobody pays to learn.

CivicAscent AI is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 42-5300126. You can read more on our About and Mission pages, see what we offer on Programs, or reach us on Contact.
