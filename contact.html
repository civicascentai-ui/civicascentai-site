What you can write to us about

You are welcome to email or call if you want help choosing a starting point, if you have a question about the free introductory learning modules, or if you want to know when community classes open. Community classes are launching December 2026. Those classes are free.

Please do not send private passwords, bank details, or other sensitive account information. If you are writing about a class, tell us the general topic and the best way to reply. We will use the information you send only to answer you and to follow up about our programs.

What to expect

We read company email as soon as we can. Response times can vary, especially around class preparation. Introductory resources on this website can be used without emailing us and without creating an account. If your question is about a class launching December 2026, we will give a clear answer about what is known so far and what is still being scheduled.

If you write in Spanish, we will do our best to reply clearly. If you need a more accessible format, tell us what would help, and we will try to accommodate you. We cannot give legal, medical, or financial advice, and we cannot repair a personal device by email. We can help you get started with everyday AI skills and tell you when a class is open.
