Privacy

How we handle information on this website

You can use our learning lessons without creating an account and without giving us personal information. You only share information with us if you choose to email or call us.

What this page covers

This is a short, plain-language description of how CivicAscent AI handles information on this website. CivicAscent AI is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 42-5300126. If you have a question after reading this page, email civicascentai@gmail.com or visit Contact.

Contact and waitlist forms on this website post to a form service. We use what you send only to answer you and to follow up about our programs or classes. We do not sell it. Please do not include passwords, bank details, or other sensitive account information.

Introductory pages

The home page includes optional settings that can make the page easier to use, such as text size and contrast. Those settings work on that page only. The page tells you that nothing is saved about you when you use those settings.

You do not need a password, a purchase, or a personal profile to explore the introductory learning resources.

When you contact us or register interest

If you email us, call us, or email us to join the class waitlist, we will receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and the question you asked. We use that information to reply and to follow up about our programs. We do not sell it.

Please do not send passwords, bank details, or other sensitive account information by email.

Questions

For privacy questions, use the company email above, or visit Contact. You can also read About, Mission, and Programs.
