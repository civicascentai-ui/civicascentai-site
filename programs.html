What you can learn with CivicAscent AI

CivicAscent AI exists to make artificial intelligence simple, practical, and free to learn for our community.

CivicAscent AI is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity. Our programs exist so everyday people can learn useful AI skills in plain language, without a technical background and without paying to learn.

Everyday AI learning modules

Free introductory learning modules are on this website now. You do not need an account, a password, or a purchase to begin. You can go slowly, repeat an activity, and come back later.

The modules explain AI in plain language and give you a chance to practice with made-up examples. Topics include:

  • What AI is, and what it is not
  • Writing a useful prompt, or question, in ordinary words
  • Using AI for planning, writing, and simple research
  • Checking AI answers for mistakes before you rely on them
  • Safer habits, including privacy and common scams

These modules are designed for beginners. They can help older adults with everyday tasks, support veterans and military families, and give families a shared starting point. You can start from the home page, open the learning path, or try the practice space.

How to get started

If you want to begin today, start with the free learning modules and practice at your own pace. If you want help choosing a starting point, or you have a question about our community classes, go to Contact or Find help. To understand why we offer this work, read our Mission.

Remember: real AI tools can make mistakes. Check important information with a reliable source before you use it.
