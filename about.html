About us

CivicAscent AI is a nonprofit that teaches everyday AI skills.

CivicAscent AI exists to make artificial intelligence simple, practical, and free to learn for our community.

What we do

We teach everyday AI skills in plain language. That means practical things a person might actually need: asking a clear question, writing a useful prompt, planning a task, drafting a message, and checking an answer for mistakes before using it.

Free introductory learning modules are on this website now. You can explore them without a purchase, a password, or a personal profile. Community classes are launching December 2026. Those classes are free. Donations support our free classes.

Who we serve

We serve everyday people who want to use AI in daily life and do not know where to start. That includes neighbors, veterans, older adults, students, workers, families, small business owners, and low-income people and communities. You do not need a technical background.

Some pages on this website are also available in Spanish. There is a page for veterans and military families.

How we teach

Simple: no jargon, and no technical talk. If a neighbor cannot follow it, we rewrite it. Practical: every lesson is about something you can actually use. Free to learn: our community classes always cost nothing. Nobody pays to learn.

We start with clear explanations, then practice with made-up examples, then a reminder to check important answers. There is no timer on the introductory pages. You can read a lesson again, take a break, and come back later. We would rather teach a few things clearly than cover everything quickly.

You can go slowly, repeat an activity, and ask for help. We also say, clearly, that AI tools can be wrong. Important information should be checked with a reliable source before anyone depends on it. This website is for learning. We do not run third-party ads here.
