Our mission

CivicAscent AI exists to make artificial intelligence simple, practical, and free to learn for our community.

CivicAscent AI is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity. EIN 42-5300126.

What that means

Our mission is one sentence, and we keep it in plain words. Behind it are three promises we make to the people we serve.

  • Simple. No jargon, and no technical talk. If a neighbor cannot follow it, we rewrite it.
  • Practical. Every lesson is about something you can actually use: writing a letter, planning a budget, helping with homework, or running a small business.
  • Free to learn. Our community classes always cost nothing. Nobody pays to learn.

We believe AI should belong to everyone, not just to people who already work in technology. Useful training should not be limited to people who already have time, money, or a technical job.

Who this is for

We serve everyday people who want to use AI in daily life and do not know where to start. That includes neighbors, veterans, older adults, students, workers, families, small business owners, and low-income people and communities. If you are new to AI, you are in the right place. If you already use some tools and want safer habits, you are also welcome.

How the mission shows up in our work

Free introductory learning modules are on this website now. You can start with a short module, take a break, and come back later. Community classes are launching December 2026. Those classes are free. Donations support our free classes.

We would rather teach a few things clearly than cover everything quickly. We measure success by whether a person leaves with a skill they can actually use, and with the habit of checking important information. Real AI tools can make mistakes, and we say so.
