Free AI learning

Start learning AI today, free.

Every lesson here is free, in plain language, and uses made-up practice examples. You do not need an account, a password, or a purchase. Go at your own pace and come back any time.

Start here

  • Clear Start: one small step at a time, with larger text, contrast and read-aloud options.
  • Level 1: AI Essentials: what AI is, how to ask clearly, and how to check an answer before you use it.
  • Five-minute starter guide: try one useful AI task in three steps.

Practice

  • Practice space: build a better prompt, catch an incorrect answer, and protect your privacy, using made-up examples.
  • Veteran career skills: describe skills from service in everyday workplace language, using made-up examples.
  • Assistive learning tools: reading and interaction options that make the lessons easier to use.

Safe habits we teach in every lesson

  • Keep real names, account numbers, passwords and money amounts out of AI tools. Keep them on paper or in your own private file.
  • AI can be wrong. Check important answers with a reliable source before you use them.
  • Use made-up examples when you practice.
